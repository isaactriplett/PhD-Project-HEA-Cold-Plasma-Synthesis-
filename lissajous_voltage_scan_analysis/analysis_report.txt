Why the model stops
  Argon 4/10/20 kHz and water 4 kHz fail passive Channel-D Q-V orientation/stability checks.
  Water 10/20 kHz and BMIM/Mn 4/10 kHz have no independent complete voltage scan.
  Although BMIM and Mn pass the passive complex-Ccell check, their 105%, 115%, and old-MAX
  Channel-D extrema do not give a monotonic positive Cd > Ccell relationship.
  Pearson current does not satisfy I = Cref*dVmonitor/dt: its gain is about 1.5-2.9 times
  the nominal 0.1-uF prediction across the old MAX groups and usually has the wrong phase.
  Pearson-derived charge is retained only as a flagged sensitivity range because an upstream
  parallel current/probe/transformer contribution and termination/scale are unresolved.

Interpretation limits
  Negative charge is an electron-plus-negative-ion equivalent; species are not separable electrically.
  The p95 half-cycle-average value is not an instantaneous nanosecond microdischarge peak.
  Charge imbalance over a 10-ms record is not retained dielectric charge.
  All nominal Channel-D charge values scale with the actual monitor-capacitor value and probe calibration.
